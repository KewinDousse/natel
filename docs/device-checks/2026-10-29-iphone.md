# 2026-10-29 · iPhone

iPhone SE, iOS 26.6.1, native Contacts and Safari. Natel 0.4.1
(`7db2936`), Radicale 3.8.3. The camera and birthday cases follow
[the recheck](ios-recheck.md); the address label and photo formats were
added.

- Taking a photo from Safari opened the crop dialog, again on a second
  try. The cropped photo showed upright in Natel and in Contacts.
- Natel showed the iOS-written 1604 birthday as July 14 without a year.
  After changing the day to 15 in Natel, then only the note in Contacts,
  the card held `BDAY;VALUE=date:1604-07-15` and Natel still showed no
  year.
- A custom address label, "Chalet", set in Natel showed in Contacts.
  Renamed "Mayen" in Contacts, it showed in Natel.
- Contacts showed a GIF photo and an EXIF-oriented portrait JPEG, both
  upright. An animated GIF stayed animated in Natel but was still in
  Contacts: the card iOS wrote back held a JPEG in its place.

Not tested: importing a `.vcf` through Safari's file picker.
