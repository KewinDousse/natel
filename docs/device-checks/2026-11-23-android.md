# 2026-11-23 · Android

OnePlus 12, OxygenOS 16.0.10 (Android 16), DAVx⁵ 4.5.21-ose, Google
Contacts 4.92.26.1020425048. Natel 0.6.0 (`18f0e9c`), Radicale 3.8.3.

Setup in Natel, on a test book: contacts "Test Alice" and "Test Bob". With
the book set to group cards, Alice was added to "Groupe Carte", which
created a vCard 3.0 group card with `X-ADDRESSBOOKSERVER-KIND:group` and
`X-ADDRESSBOOKSERVER-MEMBER`. With the book then set to categories, Bob was
added to "Groupe catégorie", which Natel wrote as `CATEGORIES` on his card.

## "Groups are separate vCards" (DAVx⁵'s default)

- "Groupe Carte" showed as a label with Alice in it, and not as a contact.
- Bob was in no "Groupe catégorie" label: this mode does not show
  `CATEGORIES`.
- Bob added to "Groupe Carte" on the phone showed as a member in Natel,
  and no longer in "Groupe catégorie": DAVx⁵ had rewritten his card
  without its `CATEGORIES`. The raw cards were read only after the whole
  check, and the server keeps no history.

## "Groups are per-contact categories"

- After switching the mode, "Groupe Carte" showed as a contact. The phone
  showed no label: Bob's `CATEGORIES` were already gone, so no category
  was left to show.
- A phone number added to that contact on the phone turned the group into
  a plain contact: DAVx⁵ rewrote the card with only `VERSION`, `UID`, `FN`,
  `N`, the number and its own `PRODID` and `REV`. `X-ADDRESSBOOKSERVER-KIND`
  and both members were gone, and Natel showed a contact "Groupe Carte".
  Alice's and Bob's cards were still on the server.
