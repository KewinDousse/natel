# 2026-10-23 · iPhone

iPhone SE, iOS 26.6.1, native Contacts and Safari. Natel 0.3.1, Radicale
3.8.3.

- Contacts displayed the JPEG, PNG and WebP test photos.
- Notes synced in both directions. Group membership, deletion, conflict
  handling and installation from Safari were checked.
- Choosing a photo from the photo library, cropping it in Safari and
  reading it back in Contacts worked.
- Taking a new photo directly from Safari returned to the form without a
  crop dialog or preview.
- iOS wrote a birthday without a year as `BDAY;VALUE=date:1604-07-14`,
  without `X-APPLE-OMIT-YEAR`, and Natel showed 1604 as the birth year
  (ADR 039).
  The exports isolate that omission before the next Natel save.
- A note edited alone in Natel retained the other fields and the Apple
  photo's bytes. Later iOS-written cards held JPEGs in place of their PNG
  and WebP photos.

Not tested: importing a `.vcf` through Safari's file picker.
